2G Phone: Accessibility Service demonstration

Android version 1.0.0 (3). Actual QA-device recordings, at normal speed, joined between the permission flow and the core-feature flow. No login is needed.

Open or download the MP4

What the video shows

  1. App opening and the app-blocking permission screen.
  2. The full prominent disclosure, choosing “Not now”, and opening the disclosure again.
  3. Affirmative consent, Android Accessibility settings, the system permission dialog, and return to the app showing “Allowed”.
  4. Core feature: Chrome has been selected for blocking. Opening Chrome redirects to 2G Phone. “Quick use” lists Chrome and explains a five-minute elapsed-time exception.

The service detects only the foreground application's package identity locally. It does not retrieve screen text, input, screenshots, or perform gestures. Selected packages, local redirect counts and quick-use deadlines are not sent to a server. Users can turn the service off in Android Accessibility settings at any time. 2G Phone is a focus app, not a disability-assistance accessibility tool.
