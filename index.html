Current test limits

These builds are being tested before their final Android feature ports are complete. App blocking, Screen Time equivalents, some alarm behavior, health or step data, notifications and on-device image analysis may be unavailable in individual apps. The store listing and test notes identify the available flow for each build.

Advertising is disabled in these Android closed-test builds. Buttons that depend on rewarded advertising are unavailable rather than granting a simulated reward.
